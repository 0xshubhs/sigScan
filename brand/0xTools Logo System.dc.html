Solidity · EVM · selector decoding · inline gas

A complete logo system for a Solidity contract-analysis tool that decodes function selectors, event topics and error selectors — and annotates gas inline — right inside VS Code. Built flat, monochrome-first, and legible from a 16px activity-bar icon up to a marquee.

02  /  Selected direction

Direction B — the Selector Strip.

Four bytes in a row, one lit — the most literal picture of the job: read a 4-byte selector and light the one under your cursor. That lit cell doubles as the inline gas annotation — unmistakably hex/EVM, with no coin and no diamond.

And it reads as data, not a badge. The byte strip is specific to selectors — where a bare 0x monogram (A) collides with the well-known 0x Protocol mark, and the reticle (C) can read as a camera. Keep the lit cell and the silhouette holds to a 16px activity-bar icon.

Matching cap heights (700 vs 698 units) let the two faces sit on one line without nudging. Both are open-source and free to ship.

DECISION · IS “0x” DIFFERENTIATED?

Yes — by typeface, not colour. The mono prefix + dotted zero reads as a literal hex prefix and survives in one ink, so it holds on a monochrome activity bar and in print. Colour-only differentiation would vanish the moment the logo goes single-ink.
