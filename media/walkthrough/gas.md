# Real-Time Gas Estimation

0xTools shows inline gas costs next to your functions and updates them as you type — no manual step required.

Estimates come from a real EVM execution backend for accuracy, falling back to the solc compiler when needed.

> **Keybinding:** Press `Cmd+K G` (macOS) or `Ctrl+K G` (Windows/Linux) to run **Estimate Gas Costs** on the active file. Toggle live annotations with **Toggle Real-time Gas Analysis**.
